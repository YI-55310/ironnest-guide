About the Game

What Is Iron Nest: Heavy Turret Simulator?

A brutal dieselpunk heavy-artillery simulator where you dominate the battlefield through a colossal war machine. Every lever, every dial, and every shot is under your control. High Command may send its orders, but the chain of command ends at your fingertips.

Set in an alternate-history 1920s Spain on the brink of civil war, you are the sole operator of a 5,000-ton artillery fortress. You read radio intel, measure a paper map, solve ballistics, load shells by hand, and crank the guns until the barrel points exactly where the math says it should.
