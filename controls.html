Remapping Advice (Do This First)

Clipboard Toggle on Caps Lock is a Trap

Mapping clipboard visibility to Caps Lock is an instantly frustrating default. You will inevitably fat-finger it during a high-stress reload - toggling your map by mistake or typing in ALL CAPS in chat later. Rebind it to Q or a thumb button on your mouse.

Gasmask Next to Pick Up is a Disaster

G (drop gasmask) sits directly next to F (pick up). Move the gasmask key further right so you don't accidentally pull your mask off while grabbing a shell during a chemical attack.

Pro Tip
Always keep the clipboard visible (Caps Lock). Even though it takes up screen space, having key information in front of you at all times is worth it.
