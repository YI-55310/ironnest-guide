Official Minimum & Recommended Specs

The developers have not published granular spec sheets; the game is a low-poly dieselpunk sim built by a two-person indie team, so it runs comfortably on modest hardware. The free Steam demo is the most reliable way to test performance on your machine - it teaches the complete firing loop and doubles as a benchmark.

Best Way to Check
Download the free demo on Steam and play 30 minutes. The game is a procedure simulator with no complex rendering load - if the demo runs smoothly, the full game will too.
