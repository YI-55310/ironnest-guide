Iron Nest All Ammo Types Guide

Around 30 unlockable shells, each forged for a single purpose. Learn the core types and which round to load for every target.

The Core Ammo Types

ShellBest AgainstNotes
AP (Armor-Piercing)Bunkers, supply depots, fire direction centersPrecision round. Best when friendlies are close or collateral must be avoided.
HE / HCH (High-Explosive)Soft targets, infantry, area effectBlast radius can damage friendlies - check the blast radius calculator first.

ChemicalArea denialExists in the arsenal - the game deliberately makes you feel bad about using it.

How to Choose the Right Round

1

Check the Mission Order

High Command often specifies the required shell in the teletype. Follow the order when it does.

2

Match the Target Class

  • Bunker / hardened structure → AP. (Use AP for underground targets too.)
  • Infantry / soft surface targets → HE or HCH.
  • Friendly units nearby → AP or check blast radius before HE.
3

Mind the Consequences

HE and HCH rounds can damage friendlies within their blast radius. Every shell is forged for a single purpose - whatever you choose will echo across the battlefield.

Ammunition Management Rules

  • Each rotary shell magazine holds six rounds - keep an eye on your stock.
  • Powder charges cannot be returned once dispensed; a shell cannot be unloaded once rammed.
  • Plan your fire missions before firing - especially with enemy artillery present, since counter-battery fire begins after your first round impacts.
  • Request aerial photography through the requisition bench when you need more eyes - photos are placed on your map automatically.
Friendly Fire
If friendlies sit close to the target, precision matters more than firepower. Confirm the specified shell type before loading - a wrong round can turn a surgical strike into an avoidable mess.
